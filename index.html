    Why this shape

    Built for small, sophisticated sites

    The Toshiba lesson: over 5,000 OEM sites worldwide, averaging under 20 agents — but with skills routing and real sophistication. A tidy agentic package that installs quick fits exactly that shape of site.

    Humans get rarer, not replaced

    As the slider moves right, agents absorb the routine volume. People concentrate where they are scarcest and best: exceptions, empathy, and judgment — the backup, not the front line.

    The market opening

    The CCaaS space is crowded and getting completely twisted up with AI1. AI2 wipes the board clean — legacy, UCaaS and AI1 alike. A slider product rides that transition instead of fighting it.

    Run lean: build and market it with no other employees required. Sites start where they are, slide up when they’re ready — and the recurring revenue builds with every stage.
